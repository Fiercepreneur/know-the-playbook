## What Is This?

This is a free, open, interactive dashboard breaking down **Project 2025 — Mandate for Leadership**, the 920-page Heritage Foundation blueprint being actively implemented by the Trump administration.

Over **53% of Project 2025 is already law or executive policy** as of February 2026.

This dashboard was built for Black Americans who need to understand what's in this document, what has already been done, and — most importantly — **what to do about it right now.**

---

## What's Inside

### ⚡ Prepare & Protect *(Start Here)*
Six threat areas with side-by-side breakdowns of the danger and concrete steps you can take today:
- Federal Jobs & Civil Service (Schedule F)
- Education & Schools (Title VI, HBCUs, student loans)
- Health & Reproductive Rights (HHS, CDC, maternal mortality)
- Financial Protection (CFPB, SBA, predatory lending)
- Civil Rights & Legal Protection (DOJ, voting rights)
- Black Immigrants & Community Safety (ICE, TPS, USAID)

Includes direct links to the **NAACP Legal Defense Fund, ACLU, vote.org, Know Your Rights cards**, and Black immigrant rights organizations.

Featured voices and outlets to stay informed from trusted sources — Joy Reid, Don Lemon, The Clay Cane Show, Black Women's News Media, AFRO News, Black Enterprise, and more.

## Key Facts

- **53%** of Project 2025's domestic agenda enacted within the first 12 months *(Center for Progressive Reform, Feb 2026)*
- **238,000** federal jobs eliminated — agencies with highest Black employee concentrations cut deepest
- **USAID** reduced from 4,900 to 370 employees — 92.4% gone
- **CFPB** gutted by 87% — predatory lending now essentially unpoliced
- **DEI** banned government-wide by executive order on Day 1
- **300,000+** Black women left the labor force by June 2025

## How To Use This

1. **Start on the ⚡ Prepare & Protect tab** — this is the action guide
2. Use the **section tabs** to dig into specific policy areas that affect you
3. **Green checkmarks** = already happening. **Red** = still coming or in court
4. Share the link widely — knowledge is the first line of defense
