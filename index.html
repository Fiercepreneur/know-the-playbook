Federal Jobs & Civil Service
300,000+ Black women already left the labor force by June 2025
⚠ ACTIVE NOW
The Threat
!
Schedule F is law. Tens of thousands of career federal positions can now be terminated at will, with no civil service appeal rights. Black federal employees are most exposed.
!
DOGE-led mass layoffs already cut 238,000 federal jobs in 2025. Agencies with the highest Black employee concentrations saw the deepest cuts.
!
DEI hiring, mentorship, and promotion programs have been eliminated government-wide. No more affirmative equity in federal contracting or hiring.
✔ How to Protect Yourself
✔
Document everything. Keep records of all performance reviews, commendations, and communications. If terminated, documentation is your legal foundation.
✔
Know your union rights. Federal unions like AFGE still provide legal representation. Join or stay active. Unions have successfully blocked many firings in court.
✔
Build private-sector transferable skills now. Certifications, LinkedIn presence, and private-sector networking are your insurance policy. Don't wait.
✔
Contact your EEOC office immediately if you face discrimination. File before any 45-day pre-complaint counseling window closes — deadlines are strict.

Education & Schools
Dept. of Education staff cut 43% · Title VI enforcement gutted · Head Start at risk
⚠ ACTIVE NOW
The Threat
!
Title VI civil rights enforcement is crippled. The OCR office that investigates racial discrimination complaints in schools has been decimated. Complaints will go unanswered.
!
Student loan programs under attack. Black borrowers hold the highest average debt. Income-driven repayment programs and forgiveness pathways are being eliminated or frozen.
!
HBCUs are vulnerable. Without a functioning Education Department, grants, accreditation support, and Title III funding for HBCUs face disruption.
✔ How to Protect Yourself
✔
Screenshot and save any student loan correspondence. Log into studentaid.gov and save your loan history, repayment plan terms, and any forgiveness program enrollment NOW.
✔
Support and enroll in HBCUs. They are under threat but fighting back. Their endowments, alumni giving, and private partnerships are their lifelines — invest in them directly.
✔
Get involved in local school boards. With federal oversight weakening, local control matters more than ever. Run, vote, and attend meetings. This is where the fight moves next.
✔
File school discrimination complaints NOW — don't wait. Use state civil rights offices as a backup when federal OCR is unresponsive. Document every incident with dates and names.
3
Health, Reproductive Rights & HHS
Black maternal mortality 2–3× higher · CDC/NIH gutted · FACE Act enforcement ended
⚠ ACTIVE NOW
The Threat
!
CDC and NIH have been gutted. HHS cut 10,000 jobs. Programs tracking racial health disparities have been purged. Data that proves discrimination in healthcare is being erased.
!
Abortion access cut for military and veterans. TRICARE and VA now cannot cover abortion care even in emergencies, rape, or incest — finalized Dec 31, 2025.
!
Medicaid under threat. Project 2025 proposes converting Medicaid to block grants — reducing coverage and flexibility in states with high Black populations.
✔ How to Protect Yourself
✔
Know your state's abortion laws NOW. Use resources like abortionfinder.org and plan ahead. If you're in a state with bans, identify the nearest legal state and clinic before you need it.
✔
Find a Black-led or culturally competent OB/GYN. Black women with Black doctors have measurably better maternal outcomes. Seek doulas, midwives, and birth advocates now.
✔
Enroll in ACA plans during open enrollment. Protect your coverage before Medicaid cuts hit. ACA marketplace plans have income-based subsidies — use them while they exist.
✔
Support community health centers (FQHCs). Federally Qualified Health Centers serve regardless of ability to pay. Find and donate to your local FQHC — they are the backup system.

Financial Protection & Wealth

⚠ ACTIVE NOW
The Threat
!
CFPB is gone in all but name. 87% of staff cut, enforcement halted. Predatory lenders, payday loan companies, and discriminatory banks now operate with almost no federal oversight.
!
SBA minority contracting programs eliminated. 8(a) and other race-conscious programs that funneled federal contracts to Black-owned businesses are being dismantled.

✔
Bank Black. Move accounts to Black-owned banks and credit unions (OneUnited, Carver Federal, etc.) that are invested in community wealth, not extraction. CDFI banks are FDIC-insured.

✔
Build a 6-month emergency fund. The safety net is being cut. Job loss, medical bills, and economic shocks have less federal backstop. Savings are your personal floor.
✔
Invest in Black-owned businesses. Mutual aid, local Black business purchasing, and community investment funds build economic power that policy cannot easily strip.

Civil Rights & Legal Protection
DOJ Civil Rights redirected · Voting protections weakened · Independent courts under pressure
⚠ ACTIVE NOW
The Threat
!
DOJ Civil Rights Division reoriented. Now focused on prosecuting perceived "anti-white discrimination." Voting rights, police misconduct, and fair housing enforcement are deprioritized.
!
Voting rights at state level. Without federal Voting Rights Act enforcement, states are accelerating gerrymandering, voter roll purges, and ID laws targeting Black voters.
!
Independent inspectors general fired. 17 IGs dismissed — the watchdogs who investigate government fraud and corruption against communities of color. Courts have not fully reinstated them.
✔ How to Protect Yourself
✔
Know your voting rights cold. Register early, check your registration status monthly at vote.org. Know your state's ID laws, early voting windows, and provisional ballot rights.
✔
Support voting rights orgs. NAACP Legal Defense Fund, Lawyers' Committee for Civil Rights, and local voter protection coalitions are fighting these battles in court. Fund and volunteer.
✔
Connect with civil rights attorneys. ACLU, NAACP LDF, and local legal aid societies provide free representation. Know your nearest office before you need it.
✔
Run for local office. School boards, city councils, county commissioners, and state legislatures are the real ground of resistance. These seats shape daily life and are winnable now.

!

✔
If you or family members are undocumented, consult an immigration attorney NOW. Understand DACA status, TPS renewal deadlines, and green card timelines before ICE makes the decision for you.
✔
Build community rapid-response networks. Know your neighbors. Set up phone trees and group chats. Create systems to alert each other of enforcement activity in real time.
✔
Support Black immigrant-led organizations. BAJI (Black Alliance for Just Immigration), UndocuBlack Network, and local immigrant rights orgs need funding, volunteers, and visibility now.

What This Means for Black America
✔
Schedule F threatens Black federal jobs. Black Americans make up ~18% of the federal workforce — far above their share of the private sector. Mass reclassification of career employees as "at-will" strips civil service protections that shield workers from political and racial discrimination.
✔
DEI offices eliminated. The plan calls for dismantling all DEI offices and programs across every agency — ending equity-focused hiring, contracting preferences, and internal accountability structures.
✔
Civil rights enforcement hollowed out. By installing political loyalists throughout agencies, the plan would sideline career officials who enforce anti-discrimination statutes in hiring, contracting, and services.
✔

✔
FCC & media consolidation: Weakening the FCC's ability to enforce diversity in media ownership allows further consolidation. ✔

!
FTC antitrust retreat: Pulling back on antitrust enforcement allows corporate monopolies to grow unchecked in industries — grocery, healthcare, banking, telecom — where Black consumers already face price gouging and service deserts in underserved communities.
The Deeper Danger: Unitary Executive
✔

✔

✔
No independent oversight means no independent investigation of discriminatory policing, no independent enforcement of fair housing rules, no independent protection of Black voters from intimidation — all subject to presidential veto.
!
History shows: concentrated executive power without checks has always been used against Black people first. Reconstruction ended under executive betrayal. Jim Crow was enforced by compliant officials. This structure creates the same vulnerability.
